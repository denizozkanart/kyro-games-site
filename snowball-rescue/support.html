Snowball Rescue – Support

Thanks for playing Snowball Rescue! If something isn't working or you have an idea, we'd love to hear from you.

Contact

Write to the support address shown on the App Store page; we answer within 3 working days. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the level number.

How to play

  • Tap the front magazine of a column to load it into a blaster; its cubes fly onto the sculpture where their colour fits.
  • The glowing spots on the sculpture show where cubes can go right now.
  • A magazine with nowhere to fire parks in one of the five slots. If all five are full, you are out of space.
  • Stuck? Use a booster: Extra Slot, Crane (lifts a parked magazine back) or Shuffle.

Common questions

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your phone. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Do I need an account or internet? No. The game works offline and has no sign-in.
