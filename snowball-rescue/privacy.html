Snowball Rescue – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-25 (game version 1.2) · Developer: Kyro Games (Deniz Özkan, sole proprietorship, Türkiye) · Contact: deniz@kyrogames.com.tr

Snowball Rescue is a puzzle game for iPhone and iPad published by Kyro Games ("we", "us"). The short version:

  • No account. You never sign in, and we never ask for your name, e-mail, phone number or contacts.
  • Your progress stays on your device. Levels, coins, boosters, lives and settings are saved in the app's own storage and are removed when you delete the app.
  • Anonymous game statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase) help us balance the levels and fix errors.
  • Ads (Unity LevelPlay) pay for the free game. Whether they may use your advertising identifier is your choice, asked once by iOS.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Firebase does not collect the advertising identifier. The game does not collect precise location, photos, contacts, the microphone or anything you type.

Advertising

The game shows a short video you choose to watch for a reward (for example three extra snowballs or a life) and, from level 21, now and then an ad between levels (at most one every 90 seconds, never after you buy No Ads). On iOS the game asks once, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether ads may use your device's advertising identifier. If you say no, the identifier is not shared and you still see ads, just less personalised ones. You can change your answer at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal.

In-app purchases

Coin packs, the starter pack, the Rescue Pack, booster bundles and No Ads are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and refunds; we never see your payment details. The game keeps a record of the purchases it has delivered on your device so that No Ads can be restored.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game schedules a few reminders on your device (for example "A frozen fish is waiting on Level 7"). They are created on the device; no push token or contact detail is sent to us. You can turn them off in Settings › Notifications.

Legal bases (EEA, UK, Türkiye)

We process statistics, crash reports and settings because we have a legitimate interest in running, securing and improving the game, and because it is needed to provide the game you asked for; purchases to perform the contract; personalised advertising (the advertising identifier) only with your consent given through the iOS prompt. Players in Türkiye: see also our KVKK Aydınlatma Metni.

International transfers

Google, Unity and Apple may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. They use safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live you may ask for access, correction, deletion or a copy of your data, object to processing, or withdraw consent, and you may complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye the KVKK Board). Because we do not know who you are, write to deniz@kyrogames.com.tr and we will explain how we can find data linked to your device. Deleting the app removes the random IDs and your saved progress from the device. Tracking and notifications can be switched off in iOS Settings at any time.

Children

The game is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are requested as "not directed at children".

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and kept by the service providers above under their own security measures.

Changes

If a future version changes what the game collects, we update this policy and the App Store privacy label before that version is released and change the effective date above.
