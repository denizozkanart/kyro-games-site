Snowball Rescue – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-13 (version 1.2) · Developer: Kyro Games · Contact: the support address on this site and on the App Store page

Snowball Rescue is a puzzle game for iPhone published by Kyro Games ("we", "us"). The short version:

  • No account, no ads, no tracking. You never sign in, and we never ask for your name, e-mail, phone number or contacts. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell or share any data.
  • Anonymous game statistics. From version 1.2 the game uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to learn which levels are too hard and to fix crashes. This data is not linked to your identity and is never used for advertising.
  • Your progress stays on your phone. Your level progress, settings and boosters are saved inside the app's own storage on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

What the game sends, and why

ServiceWhatWhyKept for
Firebase Analytics (Google)Game events (a level started, won, lost or left; stars; snowballs used; time in the level), app version, device model, iOS version, language, approximate country or region (worked out by Google from the IP address; the IP address itself is not stored) and a random app-instance ID that changes when you reinstall the appTo balance the difficulty and see where players stop14 months
Firebase Crashlytics (Google)Crash reports: what the app was doing when it crashed, device model and state, the level being played and a random installation IDTo find and fix crashes90 days

The game does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA) or the vendor identifier (IDFV), precise location, photos, contacts, or anything you type. There is no advertising in the game.

Google processes this data for us as a service provider, on servers that may be in the United States and other countries. See Google's Privacy Policy and Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game schedules a few reminders on your phone (for example "A frozen fish is waiting on Level 7"). They are created and stored on your device only: no push token or contact detail is sent anywhere. You can turn them off at any time in the iPhone's Settings › Notifications › Snowball Rescue.

Your choices

Because the statistics are not linked to you, we cannot tell which data is yours. Deleting the app removes the random IDs from your phone, so later data cannot be connected with earlier data. Notifications can be switched off in Settings.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children: the statistics above contain no name, e-mail or contact details and are not used for advertising or profiling.

Purchases

If a future version offers in-app purchases, they are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details.

Changes

If a future version changes what is collected, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy label before that version is released, and change the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see the support page.
