Snowball Rescue – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-23 · Developer: Kyro Games · Contact: the support address on this site and on the App Store page

Snowball Rescue is a puzzle game for iPhone published by Kyro Games ("we", "us"). The short version:

  • We collect no data. This version of the game contains no analytics, advertising, account or tracking SDK.
  • No account. You never sign in, and we never ask for your name, e-mail, phone number or contacts.
  • Your progress stays on your phone. Your level progress, settings and boosters are saved inside the app's own storage on your device and are removed when you delete the app.
  • No tracking. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell or share any data.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Purchases

If a future version offers in-app purchases, they are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details.

Changes

If a future version adds analytics or advertising, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy label before that version is released, and change the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see the support page.
