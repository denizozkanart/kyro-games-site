Blast & Build – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-15 (version 1.1) · Developer: Kyro Games · Contact: the support address on this site and on the App Store page

Blast & Build is a puzzle game for iPhone published by Kyro Games ("we", "us"). The short version:

  • No account. You never sign in, and we never ask for your name, e-mail, phone number or contacts.
  • Your progress stays on your phone. Your level, coins and boosters are saved inside the app's own storage on your device and are removed when you delete the app.
  • Anonymous game statistics and crash reports help us balance the levels and fix errors (Google Firebase).
  • Ads (Unity LevelPlay) pay for the free game. Whether they may use your advertising identifier is your choice, asked once by iOS.
  • We do not sell your data.

Game statistics and crash reports

To understand how the game is played and to fix problems, Blast & Build uses Google Firebase:

  • Firebase Analytics records anonymous game events, such as starting, winning or failing a level, a revive, using or buying a booster or claiming the daily gift, together with basic technical information (device model, operating system version, app version, country derived from the network). These events are tied to a random app-instance identifier, not to you.
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends a report when the game crashes (the state of the app and device at the time of the crash) so we can fix the error.

Firebase does not collect the device's advertising identifier. Google processes this data on our behalf under its own terms (Firebase privacy).

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game books a few reminders on your device (for example when your daily gift is ready). They are scheduled locally; nothing is sent to a server. You can turn them off in your iPhone's Settings.

Advertising

Blast & Build shows ads: a short video you choose to watch for a reward (free coins, a free revive, double coins or a free booster), and from level 8 now and then an ad between levels (never after you buy No Ads). The ads come through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource and its ad partners). To show and measure ads they receive information such as the device model and operating system, the app, the IP address (from which an approximate country or region is derived) and how you interact with the ads.

On iOS the game asks once, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether ads may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to be more relevant to you. If you say no, the identifier is not shared and you still see ads, just less personalised ones. You can change the answer at any time in your iPhone's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal.

In-app purchases

Coin packs, bundles, the starter pack and No Ads are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The game keeps a record of the purchases it has delivered on your device.

Children

Blast & Build is meant for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The game does not ask for a name, e-mail or any other contact details.

Changes

If a future version changes what the game collects, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy label before that version is released, and change the effective date above.
